Use skills/film-review/SKILL.md. Your task is to judge the actual candidate as a complete product launch, not defend its construction. Work only within the assigned evidence packet and output scope. Read raw visual/audiovisual inputs before producer explanations. Use the provided original brief and real references; do not read unrelated history to infer an expected verdict.
